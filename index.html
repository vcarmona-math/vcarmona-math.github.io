About me

I am a postdoc at the Institut Montpelliérain Alexander Grothendieck (IMAG), working with Damien Calaque. Prior to that I was a postdoc at MPI-MiS Leipzig working in the research group "Mathematical Structures in Physics", and even before I was a PhD student at the University of Seville under the supervision of Fernando Muro and Ramon Flores. My research interests are somehow broad; one might say, homotopy theory and its tubular neighborhood (whatever this means).

I have/had the pleasure of working on different projects together with: L. Alonso · A. Jeremías, K. Arakawa · F. Pratali, J. Aramayona · F. Cantero · J.J. Gutiérrez, J. Millès · S. Yalin, M. Benini · A. Grant-Stuart · P. Naaijkens · A. Schenkel, D. Calaque, F. Muro · R. Flores, and A. Švraka.

A few keywords: higher categories and higher algebra, [pr(op]erads), Goodwillie and manifold calculus, factorization algebras, quantum field theory...
